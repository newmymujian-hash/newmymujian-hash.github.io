How to read this page

Each figure is the estimated net flow into sector ETFs on the most recent trading day, in the market’s own currency. Red is a net inflow, green a net outflow — the A-share market convention, applied to both markets here so the two panels read the same way.

The ◆ marker flags an extreme flow day: a sector whose daily net flow, in absolute value, exceeds the 95th percentile of its own daily flows over the past year.

Method and sources

Net flow is self-calculated as (shares outstanding today − shares outstanding yesterday) × today’s NAV, summed over the ETFs in each sector. It is an estimate of primary-market creations and redemptions, not exchange turnover, and it can differ from commercial data vendors’ figures, which use their own conventions.

  • China A-shares: shares from the SSE / SZSE daily ETF scale reports (T-day close); NAV from Eastmoney. Data for trading day T is finalised on the morning of T+1.
  • United States: shares and NAV from issuer daily files (State Street SPDR; VanEck for SMH). U.S. shares dated T conventionally reflect creation/redemption orders through T−1.
  • Change % is the daily change reported in the source data (for China A-share ETFs this is the NAV change).
  • ETF labels on this page are English renderings of the exchange short names; the codes are authoritative.

Data is compiled automatically after each market close and is shown for information only. It is not investment advice.
