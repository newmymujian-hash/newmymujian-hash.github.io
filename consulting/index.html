Consulting

Professional background in management consulting — credentials and areas of practice.

Background

Economist with a PhD and roughly twenty years in finance: university lecturer, credit-rating analyst, executive director in a securities firm’s fixed-income department, and founder of a credit-rating company. Now based in Calgary, working in information and risk services for small and medium-sized enterprises.

Credential

Certified Management Consultant (CMC) — the profession’s international certification mark, administered in Canada by CMC-Canada and conferred by the provincial institutes. Holders commit to the CMC Uniform Code of Professional Conduct.

Areas of practice

  • Corporate strategy and market entry
  • Credit-rating methodology and risk-management systems
  • Cross-border business involving the Chinese market
  • Market intelligence and risk assessment for SMEs

Selected experience

  • Developed rating methodologies, including an internal methodology series for a major state-owned enterprise group, at China Chengxin International
  • Advised financial institutions on interest-rate bond investment and macroeconomic research (since 2021)
  • Led the design and operation of an automated rating information system
