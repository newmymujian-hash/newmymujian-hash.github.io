Credential: Certified Management Consultant (CMC)

Jian Mu is a Certified Management Consultant (CMC), admitted to membership by the Institute of Certified Management Consultants of Alberta on July 13, 2026. The CMC designation is Canada’s recognized benchmark for management consulting competence and ethics: holders have satisfied the Institute’s professional qualification requirements and have agreed to practise in complete accordance with its code of professional conduct and by-laws.

Conseiller en management certifié (C.M.C.) — Institute of Certified Management Consultants of Alberta.
