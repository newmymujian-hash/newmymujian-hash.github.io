Background

Before finance, I taught at university level — quantitative courses including microeconomics, macroeconomics, technical economics, and operations research. Teaching is where I started, and I still think like a teacher: concepts first, then layered practice.

Study resources (free)

  • Physics
  • Chemistry

Approach

  • Build the model before drilling the procedure
  • Worked examples first, then problems in ascending difficulty
  • Use mathematics competitions to pull language and reasoning skills along with it
