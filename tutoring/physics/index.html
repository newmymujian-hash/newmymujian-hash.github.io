Physics

Catalogue of the physics teaching library: Alberta Physics 30 course pack, AP Physics, lesson plans, and a large exam bank — 344 files in total.

The physics library holds 344 files (about 533 MB), organised by course and material type. This page is the catalogue; individual files are being selected for open publication in stages.

Alberta Physics 30 course pack (81 files)

A complete Sir Winston Churchill / Calgary Board of Education Physics 30 package — slides, booklets with answer keys, and course forms, all present and accounted for:

  • Course management & forms — course outline, formula sheet, lab-report guidelines, lab safety and academic-integrity forms
  • Momentum — booklet and key, slide deck
  • Electricity & magnetism — electrostatics and magnetism booklets with keys, slide decks
  • Light & optics (17 items) — wave review, EMR, rectilinear propagation, speed of light, reflection, curved mirrors, refraction, lenses, colour and dispersion, diffraction, polarization
  • EMR & atomic structure (16 items) — Thomson, Millikan, Planck, photoelectric effect, X-rays, Rutherford, spectra, Bohr model, Compton effect, de Broglie, wave–particle duality
  • Nuclear & particle physics (17 items) — quantum model of the atom, radioactivity and decay rates, fission & fusion, subatomic particles, quarks and the Standard Model

AP Physics (16 files)

  • AP Physics 1 — ten units as PDFs: kinematics; laws of motion; work & mechanical energy; gravitation; momentum & collisions; rotational motion; oscillations; waves; electric forces & DC circuits; DC circuits
  • AP Physics 2 — electric force/energy & DC circuits, thermal physics, magnetic fields — PDFs plus narrated slide decks

Lesson plans & handouts (18 files)

Force analysis, kinematics, uniform circular motion, electrostatics, torque balance, work & energy — teaching plans and illustrated exercise sheets (in Chinese, for the Chinese senior-high curriculum), plus English kinematics practice sets.

Exam bank (145 items)

  • University entrance exams — China’s national and provincial papers, centred on the full 2021 set (national papers A/B, Zhejiang, Hebei, Hunan, Guangdong, Jiangsu, with solutions and high-resolution editions), plus the complete 1952–1989 national exam collection and a Taiwan entrance paper
  • Proficiency & graduation exams — Beijing, Guangxi, Hunan and Anhui papers
  • Competitions — national physics competition papers and simulations
  • School exams & unit tests — incl. the High School Affiliated to Renmin University opening exam; monthly, term and unit tests across mechanics, electromagnetism and gravitation
  • Numbered problem banks & topical handouts — the 33/34/35/3720/3821 series; torque, mechanical energy, vibration, kinematics, statics, projectile and circular motion sets
  • Textbooks & references — the national senior-high physics textbook (Compulsory Book 2), teacher’s books, a formula compendium and a knowledge-system summary
  • In English — the official AP Physics C course and exam description, kinematics practice and solutions (Univ. of Manitoba PHYS 1120), and a physics-education research paper

Catalogue status: the library is fully inventoried (November 2026). Copyrighted textbooks and third-party exam compilations are listed for reference; openly licensed and original materials will be published first as the selection is made.
