About

Jian Mu (Alex) — economist and credit-market specialist in Calgary.

Profile

Economist and credit-market specialist with over ten years of rigorous academic training in macroeconomics, consumption theory, and financial theory, and more than fifteen years of professional experience across credit rating, bond issuance, interest-rate bond investment, and macroeconomic research. Published seven academic papers, including core-journal and CSSCI-indexed articles.
