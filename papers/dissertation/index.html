The Transformation of Consumption Patterns in China's Marketization Process

PhD dissertation, Renmin University of China (2011), supervised by Zhenhua Mao. Full English translation: about 99,000 words.

Jian Mu (Alex) · Nov 3, 2026 · 1 min read

PhD dissertation, Renmin University of China, 2011. Supervisor: Professor Zhenhua Mao.

This dissertation studies how household consumption in China changed as the economy moved from cash-flow-based consumption — spending constrained by current income — toward a balance-sheet pattern in which assets, credit, and expectations shape spending. It traces the formation of the cash-flow pattern, its persistence in the early reform period, the foundations and paths of the transition, and the risks and obstacles of the balance-sheet pattern, drawing on international experience.

The full English translation (about 99,000 words, seven chapters plus references) has been completed for this site, with all 69 tables and 209 formulas and figures restored from the original manuscript. Chapters will be published here progressively.
