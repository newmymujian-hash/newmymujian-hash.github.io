China's Low Consumption Rate and Stage-Based Household Expenditure

Lanzhou Academic Journal, 2010. Full English translation available.

Jian Mu (Alex) · Nov 3, 2026 · 1 min read

Lanzhou Academic Journal (兰州学刊), 2010.

This paper examines why China’s household consumption rate stayed low, through the lens of stage-based household expenditure: spending needs arrive in stages across the life cycle, and when several stages bunch together, households compress current consumption. It is the journal version of the flagship paper of my consumption research line; the same study appeared in Contemporary Finance & Economics (当代财经, CSSCI) in 2010.

The full English translation (about 6,000 words, including both tables) is complete and will be linked here.
